Our GVF Memories

Three unforgettable shows, one very happy heart.

Photo placeholder
Dreams in Gold Tour

Bangor, Maine — October 8, 2022

Cross Insurance Center

Our first Greta Van Fleet concert together at the Cross Insurance Center in Bangor, Maine. This was the Dreams in Gold Tour, and it was unforgettable - though for unexpected reasons. During this show, Josh Kiszka ruptured his eardrum mid-performance, which led to the postponement of several tour dates. We were there to witness rock and roll history, even if we didn't know it at the time!

Photo placeholder
Starcatcher World Tour

Niagara Falls, Ontario — August 4, 2024

OLG Stage at Fallsview Casino

An intimate show at the OLG Stage at Fallsview Casino during their Starcatcher World Tour. With only about 400 GA tickets sold, this was a special experience - close to the stage and feeling every note. Crown Lands opened, and the venue's acoustics made every song resonate. This was summer magic.

Photo placeholder
Sommo Festival 2024

Cavendish, PEI — September 14, 2024

Sommo Festival

Greta Van Fleet closed out Sommo Festival 2024 in Cavendish, Prince Edward Island. With over 16,000 fans in attendance, this was the biggest crowd we'd experienced at a GVF show. The energy was electric, the pyrotechnics were incredible, and watching them headline a festival in the Maritimes was a perfect way to end our summer of concerts.

Made with all my love for you, Marice.
